# CV, recommendation and interview preparation

**CV.** Align to the degree and length limit. State roles, tools and outcomes with dates, exact ownership and available evidence. Separate team deliverables and individual work. Describe assisted implementation and what the applicant independently verified where relevant.

**Recommenders.** Map required type to a person with direct observation of specific work. Distinguish direct observations from applicant-provided context, confirm availability and use authorised channels. Leave authorship and submission to the recommender. Store status and purpose without private contact details or raw letters.

**Interview.** Prepare both a plain-language explanation and a technical explanation for each key experience. Ask follow-up questions about assumptions, dataset, benchmark, method, outcome, limitations and the applicant's decisions. The applicant should be able to explain it independently. Follow programme rules for any live or recorded interview and track gaps for preparation.
