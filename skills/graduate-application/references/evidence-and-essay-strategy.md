# Evidence mapping and essay strategy

For every prompt record: what it asks, what the applicant needs the reader to understand, one primary story and an alternate if needed, the actual individual decision or contribution, related claim IDs, permitted scope, and open questions. Check with the owner before using reconstructed dialogue, feelings, motives or unconfirmed results.

Read the entire essay set before reviewing a single draft. Record the function of each essay and the overlap in events, conclusions and emotional beats. Give repeated projects a distinct function or use a different story. Use optional essays for a specific unresolved explanation.

Check the programme's AI policy before generating any outline, draft, translation, rewrite or interview aid. When only brainstorming and grammar checks are allowed, surface relevant facts, open questions and factual mismatches and leave the final structure and narrative to the applicant. Track required use disclosures. Link to the current draft and record authorship, date and review scope.
