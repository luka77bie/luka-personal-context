# Programme selection and research

1. Record entry year, degree, delivery mode, track, location, duration, cost constraints and what remains undecided. Identify the applicant's current objective, exploratory interests and longer-term possibilities.
2. Assess fit by tracing an existing experience → a concrete skill gap → a verified programme resource → a plausible use of the resource → the next career step. Verify that any named course, placement, centre or activity is available to the relevant degree, cohort and track.
3. Mark each item as official requirement, official positioning, anecdotal signal, or strategic inference. Official admissions instructions establish requirements. Student accounts can raise questions to verify. Interpretive fit remains provisional.
4. Keep grades in their original system. Use a conversion only if the destination specifies one. Record dates and citations for verified claims. Compare alternatives on concrete differences such as training, duration or access.
5. Output a sourced fit note, unresolved eligibility or resource questions, and an applicant decision. Keep status exploratory until the applicant confirms.
