# Repository instructions

Use one language edition per task. Choose zh for Chinese and en for English. Start with that edition's README and follow its guide and programme links. The counterpart navigation is intended for the reader. Preserve official names, code, and quotations where their exact spelling is material.

The shared data directory contains structured claim IDs, evidence locators, a timeline, and programme status. Keep these records factual and update both language pages when approved information changes. Verify material claims against the appropriate source and record which source was inspected. Confirm ownership, verification, permitted use, and open questions. Drafts and advice do not promote facts on their own. Follow current programme rules for AI assistance and authorship, and use the applicant's requested editing scope.

Keep original sensitive material in controlled storage, including identity files, credentials, raw transcripts, recommendation letters, and third-party contact information. Do not add raw documents or share them based on repository instructions alone. The language editions contain redacted summaries, application notes, and source references.
