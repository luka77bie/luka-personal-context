# Personal and academic statement review

[Home](../README.md) · [简体中文](../../zh/workflows/ps-review.md)

Start with the [Graduate application method](../skills/graduate-application/SKILL.md), then apply these review steps.

1. Establish the programme, entry year, prompt, draft and requested feedback. Read the programme brief and check relevant official requirements. Record the materials inspected.
2. Establish the permitted assistance. When independent structure and writing are required, preserve the applicant’s structure. Ask material questions, verify facts or flag wording issues within the permitted scope. Identify required disclosures.
3. Read relevant experience records, claims and discrepancies. Verify roles, dates, results and allowed use. Identify outstanding details that could change the interpretation.
4. Give specific feedback with its source. Where substantive editing is permitted, track material factual changes paragraph by paragraph. Use questions when the applicant must write the text.
5. Check prompt fit, length, voice, causal language, programme fit and attribution.
6. Deliver the sources used, priority issues, permitted feedback, reasons for changes and remaining decisions. The applicant approves and submits.

Example request. “Read AGENTS.md, START_HERE.md and the relevant programme brief. Review my draft within the programme’s rules. Prioritise prompt fit, facts and my own voice.”
