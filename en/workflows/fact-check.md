# Fact-checking workflow

[Home](../README.md) · [简体中文](../../zh/workflows/fact-check.md)

1. Locate a claim ID or experience record for each material statement. Identify applicant accounts, working drafts, official records and reproducible results in the source catalogue.
2. Check the assertion, evidence strength and allowed use separately. Verify formal marks against official records, contributions against evidence of the work, and personal feelings with the applicant.
3. For a conflict, record both sources, the difference and the evidence needed to resolve it. Omit unresolved numbers or conclusions from formal text.
4. For research results, record the run version, sample, metric, benchmark and supported conclusion. Distinguish team and individual work.
5. Update claims and timeline after confirmation. If an earlier version was submitted, preserve it alongside a separate correction record.

For each claim, record source_type, verification_status, owner_confirmation and allowed_use. Treat draft-only material as working context. Material external claims require the appropriate evidence and applicant confirmation.
