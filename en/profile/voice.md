# Writing voice and editing preferences

[Home](../README.md) · [简体中文](../../zh/profile/voice.md)

The applicant generally prefers feedback in Chinese with English names and technical terms. He wants application writing to sound like his own thinking: concise, natural, specific and less formulaic. Start from a concrete choice, result or scene. Give the location of a sentence-level issue, explain it and suggest a specific change that preserves his meaning. Verify course or programme facts before personalising them. If a claim is unsupported, flag or narrow it instead of making it sound certain. Follow the school’s rules for structure, wording and content.

For interview practice, begin with simple questions and answers of two or three sentences. Build toward longer answers only after the logic is clear. His current practice needs are connecting ideas, retrieving vocabulary under pressure and maintaining a coherent sequence. WRT-001.
