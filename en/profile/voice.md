# Writing voice and editing preferences

[Home](../README.md) · [简体中文](../../zh/profile/voice.md)

The applicant generally prefers feedback in Chinese with English names and technical terms. Start from a concrete choice, result or scene. Give the location of a sentence-level issue, explain it and suggest a specific change that preserves his meaning. Follow the school’s rules for structure, wording and content.

| Sample category | Use |
| --- | --- |
| Applicant’s original wording | Use for voice. Record context, date and applicant confirmation. An independent English sample is still needed |
| AI rewrite awaiting confirmation | Use for comparison and review |
| Wording approved by the applicant | Record the approved wording, context, source and assistance |
| Adviser feedback | Identify the scope of the editorial suggestion |
| Another applicant’s sample | Use for genre or prompt context. Keep it separate from his facts, stories and voice samples |

Record the sample, language, purpose, author, AI assistance, confirmation date and why he liked or disliked it. Confirm authorship and revision history for earlier statements and the adviser packet.
