# Discrepancies and open checks

[Home](../README.md) · [简体中文](../../zh/facts/conflicts.md)

| Item | Available sources | Treatment |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Housing mark | English CV lists a module mark of 75%. A design note reports 72 in a questionnaire. The adviser packet omits a figure because the definitions differ. | Check coursework feedback and module transcript. Omit the mark until the distinction is verified. |
| Predicted degree | CV and adviser packet say expected 2:1. A design note attributes a personal First-Class estimate to an older Chinese CV. | Record personal targets, achieved marks and official predictions separately. An institutional prediction requires university evidence. |
| Founder dates and scope | English CV lists July–August 2026. An earlier applicant account gives 5–18 July. The packet separates the placement and independent study. | Use summer 2026 in working drafts until the certificate establishes exact dates. Attribute the out-of-sample extension to independent research. |
| Peer mentoring remit | The packet reports 19 mentors. Group numbers and register definitions require reconciliation. | Describe coordination duties. Check total scheme headcount, individual remit and completed allocations separately. |
| Cornell concentration | The packet suggests Technology Management. An SOP draft names Behavioural Finance. | Confirm his choice and the concentrations available for 2027 before citing one. |
| Quant Research Lab | A design note mentions an earlier version and an update on 8 September 2026. | Check each study’s latest report and run. The ETF study has identifiable source material. |
| Writing sample authorship | The division of applicant writing and subsequent editing is unverified. | Label drafts as working material. Obtain a sample written independently by the applicant. |
| Duke degree conferral and transcript | Fuqua lists 25 June for a transcript showing a conferred degree. The applicant expects to graduate in 2027. Manchester’s exact issue date is pending. | Confirm conferral and issue dates with Manchester, then confirm any provisional or alternative documents with Fuqua. |
| Career sequence | On 19 November he prioritised equity research and company fundamentals, then multi-asset research. On 22 November interview practice named private equity as a future goal. | Keep equity research as the confirmed immediate priority and private equity as exploratory until he explicitly reconciles the sequence. |
