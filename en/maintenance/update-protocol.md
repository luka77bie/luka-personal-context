# Updating the records

[Home](../README.md) · [简体中文](../../zh/maintenance/update-protocol.md)

1. Identify the affected record, source type, location and event date. This repository is public. Keep identity files, addresses, contact details, credentials, private file URLs, raw recommendations and confidential institutional documents in controlled storage.
2. Follow [Fact-checking workflow](../workflows/fact-check.md) to record source type, verification, applicant confirmation and allowed use. Verify grades and predictions from official records and ask the applicant about reflections and motives.
3. Record conflicting accounts in the [Discrepancy log](../facts/conflicts.md) with both sources. Once confirmed, update claims, timeline and experience records, then refresh the relevant context summaries.
4. Keep programme requirements, drafts, reviews and submitted versions together. Mark progress as exploring, drafting, reviewed, approved or submitted according to the evidence. Record submission date, version, channel and applicant confirmation.
5. Update the [Change history](../CHANGELOG.md). Distinguish the event, recording and verification dates. Track the basis for major factual changes and retain superseded versions as needed.
