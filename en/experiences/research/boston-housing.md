# Greater Boston housing price regression

[Home](../../README.md) · [简体中文](../../../zh/experiences/research/boston-housing.md)

**Scope.** Individual Manchester Applied Statistics coursework, 2025/26. Sources SRC-CV and SRC-CORNELL-SOP. Claim HOU-001.

**Question.** How do housing prices relate to school resources across 152 Greater Boston towns? The study compared the associations for per-pupil expenditure and class size as controls were added.

**Work.** Cleaned and explored the data in R, log-transformed right-skewed variables and fitted three nested OLS models. Controls included income, education, tax and distance. Compared coefficients, standard errors, significance, fit and residuals. The CV reports a maximum VIF of 2.96. Check the report before quoting it.
