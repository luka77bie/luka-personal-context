# Fitness transformation and marathon preparation

[Home](../../README.md) · [Experience index](../index.md) · [简体中文](../../../zh/experiences/activities/fitness-and-marathon.md)

## Context

Jiacheng reports that he began training systematically at university and lost about 20 kilograms over roughly three to four months. He later described a routine of training about five times a week. This is a personal account of sustained behaviour rather than an academic or professional achievement.

## Endurance goal

He reports completing the Manchester Half Marathon in about 2 hours 24 minutes. After a period with little running, he began rebuilding his aerobic base while continuing strength training and registered for the 2027 Manchester Marathon.

## What the episode can show

The strongest application use is the process: setting a demanding goal, maintaining a routine and restarting gradually after a lapse. It can support interview questions about discipline, self-direction or a long-term commitment. Avoid turning weight change into a general claim about performance in unrelated settings.

## Evidence and boundaries

Claims FIT-001 and FIT-002. Source SRC-CHAT-FITNESS. The weight change, usual training frequency, half-marathon result and marathon registration are owner-reported. Confirm the official race result and event date if a formal application needs them. Do not add medical details, exact current body measurements or body-image claims without explicit approval.
