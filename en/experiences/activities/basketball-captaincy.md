# High-school basketball captaincy

[Home](../../README.md) · [Experience index](../index.md) · [简体中文](../../../zh/experiences/activities/basketball-captaincy.md)

## Context

In his final high-school league season, Jiacheng was captain, starting centre and one of the team’s main scorers. When the coach became unavailable shortly before the competition, his working application material says he also took responsibility for rotations and tactics.

## Turning point

The team lost its opening match by three points and players began blaming one another. Jiacheng’s account says he concluded that the offence relied too heavily on interior scoring and did not make enough use of other players’ speed and outside shooting. For the next match, he voluntarily stepped out of the starting lineup so that he could observe the opposing defence, reconsider roles and organise the team from the sideline.

## Outcome and reflection

His account records two wins in the following matches and improved offensive organisation and defensive discipline before the team was eliminated in the knockout stage. The sequence does not establish that the lineup change alone caused the wins.

The episode is useful because his decision reduced his own visibility and playing time in order to reconsider what the team needed. His current reflection is that leadership should be judged by contribution to the shared task, not only by being the most visible performer.
