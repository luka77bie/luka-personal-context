**Entry year** 2027. Public information checked on 21 November 2026. Confirm exact portal prompts and the applicant’s round.

- **Academic Statement of Purpose.** Up to 500 words covering academic interests, preparation and training, reasons for this programme and career goals.
- **Personal Statement.** In 300–500 words, explain how background and experience influenced the decision and preparation for graduate study.
- **AI assistance.** Cornell Graduate School’s 1 September 2026 update permits topic brainstorming and spelling or grammar checks and requires disclosure of GenAI use. The applicant must produce the outline, draft and substantive content. Large-scale AI translation is prohibited. Flag grammar issues for the applicant to revise and check additional programme rules.
- **Available records.** SRC-CORNELL-PS and SRC-CORNELL-SOP. See [Programme fit](fit.md) and [Open checks](../../../facts/conflicts.md).
