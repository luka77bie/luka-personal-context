# UCL MSc Finance personal-statement structure

[Application index](../../index.md) · [简体中文](../../../../../zh/applications/2027-entry/programmes/ucl-mfin/structure.md)

This is the applicant-confirmed five-part outline for the UCL MSc Finance personal statement:

1. Simplify the ICBC opening.
2. Retain the Greater Boston housing project.
3. Use Founder Securities to lead into the independent ETF research.
4. Explain what he wants to learn at UCL.
5. Finish with the career plan.

Dragon Rise is not an approved sixth paragraph. Keep the outline separate from unconfirmed programme-specific claims.
