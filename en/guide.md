# Application Workflow

[Home](README.md) · [简体中文](../zh/guide.md)

1. Read the [profile](profile.md) and [target programme](applications/README.md). Read only the records relevant to the task, name the sources actually used, and ask for details that materially affect the outcome.
2. Check the exact prompt, limits, round, time zone, recommendation requirements, and AI policies for the same entry cycle. Distinguish official requirements and positioning, personal experience, and strategic interpretation.
3. Pick a verifiable action from [stories and evidence](stories.md). Connect the experience, current skills gap, accessible programme resource, and intended career use. Check eligibility to use the resource and seek evidence before inferring admissions preferences.
4. Give each essay a distinct purpose. Respect the applicant’s editing request and the institution’s authorship rules. Keep source text, editorial suggestions, uncertainties, and applicant confirmation distinct.
5. For the CV, check dates, ownership, and results. Prepare recommendations around firsthand observations. Practise interview explanations in plain language first, then cover data, methods, assumptions, and limits.
6. Cross-check essays, CV, portal fields, and recommender materials. Record the final version and submission only when confirmed. Update drafts and facts through their own records.

Update verified facts and sources first, then sync both corresponding language pages. Include dates and supporting sources for major changes and keep a record of what was submitted. Resolve uncertain claims, redundant essay material, overstated prototype outcomes, and sensitive originals before proceeding.

## Read by task

- General background: [Profile](profile.md) and [facts and timeline](materials.md).
- Applications and topic selection: [application overview](applications/README.md) and the relevant programme page.
- Technical interviews: the contribution and methods in [experience](experiences.md), with ST-01, ST-02, and ST-04.
- Community and leadership: ST-03 and ST-05 in [stories and evidence](stories.md), with firsthand interactions and feedback.
